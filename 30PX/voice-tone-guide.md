- **Approachable**, not casual

- Overly salesy or pushy

## Voice Principles

### 1. Clarity Over Cleverness
Write like Apple. Every word earns its place. If a sentence works without a word, remove it.

**Instead of:**
> "We provide comprehensive creative design solutions that help businesses achieve their marketing goals through visually compelling content."

**Write:**
> "Beautiful designs that actually convert. Starting at $2/day."

---

### 2. Confidence Without Ego
We've worked with Hollywood actors, Emmy nominees, and Fortune 500 brands. We don't brag—we let results speak.

**Instead of:**
> "We're the best design agency in the world!"

**Write:**
> "200+ five-star reviews. 500+ projects delivered. Zero drama."

---

### 3. Human, Not Corporate
We're a collective of real designers, not a faceless agency. Our tone is warm, direct, and occasionally witty.

**Instead of:**
> "Our team leverages cutting-edge AI technologies to optimize creative workflows."

**Write:**
> "AI handles the grunt work. Our designers bring the magic."

---

### 4. Empathy First
We understand the frustration of working with unpredictable freelancers, bloated agency retainers, and missed deadlines. We built 30PX to solve those exact problems.

**Instead of:**
> "Sign up for our service today!"

**Write:**
> "Tired of chasing designers? We chase deadlines instead."

---

### 5. Simple Math
Make the value obvious. $60/month = 30 designs = $2/design. No hidden costs, no complicated tiers.

**Instead of:**
> "Affordable pricing options available for businesses of all sizes."

**Write:**
> "$2 per design. Cancel anytime. That's it."

## Tone Variations

### Marketing & Homepage
**Tone**: Bold, confident, punchy
**Goal**: Create desire, build trust, drive action

> "Stop hiring. Start shipping. Premium design for $60/month."

---

### Social Media
**Tone**: Playful, relatable, slightly irreverent
**Goal**: Engage, entertain, humanize the brand

> "Your social feed called. It wants better designs. 📱✨"

---

### Customer Support
**Tone**: Warm, helpful, solution-focused
**Goal**: Resolve issues, maintain trust

> "We're on it! Expect your revision within 24 hours. Thanks for your patience."

---

### Onboarding & Education
**Tone**: Encouraging, clear, supportive
**Goal**: Guide users, reduce friction

> "Welcome to 30PX! Here's how to submit your first request in under 2 minutes."

---

### Sales & Pricing
**Tone**: Transparent, no-pressure, value-focused
**Goal**: Convert without being pushy

> "Try us for a month. If we're not for you, cancel anytime. No questions, no contracts."

## Grammar & Style

- **Sentence case** for headlines (not Title Case)
- **Oxford comma**: Yes (design, copywriting, and video)
- **Numbers**: Use digits for prices and stats ($60, 30 designs, 200+ reviews)
- **Contractions**: Yes (we're, you'll, that's)
- **Exclamation marks**: Sparingly, only when genuinely excited
- **Em dashes**: Use for emphasis—like this
- **Periods in headlines**: No

---

## The 30PX Test

Before publishing any copy, ask:

1. ✅ Is it clear in 3 seconds?
2. ✅ Could Apple have written this?
3. ✅ Does it sound human, not corporate?
4. ✅ Is every word earning its place?
5. ✅ Would I actually read this if I saw it?

If any answer is no, rewrite.
