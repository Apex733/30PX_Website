# 30PX Website Sections

## Landing Page Structure

### 1. Header (Sticky)
**Elements:**
- Logo (left)
- Navigation links: Services, Pricing, Reviews, Industries
- CTA button: "Start for $60" (right)
- Mobile: Hamburger menu

---

### 2. Hero Section
**Purpose:** Create immediate value proposition and drive to pricing

**Elements:**
- Badge: "Top Rated on Fiverr • 200+ Five-Star Reviews"
- Headline (H1): Primary value proposition
- Subheadline: Supporting statement
- Primary CTA: "Start for $60/month"
- Secondary CTA: "See pricing"
- Optional: Video thumbnail or client logo strip

---

### 3. Social Proof Bar
**Purpose:** Instant credibility

**Elements:**
- "Trusted by" text
- Client logos: iFixit, Jammers, NewChic, etc.
- Stats: "500+ projects delivered"

---

### 4. Pain Points Section (Interactive)
**Purpose:** Show empathy and present solutions

**Layout:**
- Left: Chat-style clickable pain points
- Right: Dynamic response showing 30PX solution

**Pain Points:**
1. I get designs, but I don't get measurable results.
2. I'm stuck with unpredictable costs and bloated agency retainers.
3. I'm tired of managing designers instead of my business.
4. The creative process is just too slow and always misses deadlines.
5. Finding good, reliable designers is a nightmare.
6. I waste too much time explaining and going through revisions.

---

### 5. Comparison Table Section
**Purpose:** Demonstrate clear value vs alternatives

**Columns:**
1. 30PX (highlighted)

3. Freelancers
4. DIY Platforms

**Rows:**
- Cost
- Speed/Turnaround
- Quality/Talent
- Management
- Scalability
