# 30PX Voice & Tone Guide

## Who We Are

30PX is a design agency powered by top-tier AI. We are not a tool. We are not a marketplace. We are a team of veteran designers who use the most advanced AI models available (Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, Sora, Kling AI, and others) to deliver agency-quality creative work at a price that makes sense.

Our business model is simple: AI handles the heavy lifting. Our designers handle the thinking. The client gets premium results without the premium invoice.

---

## Core Message

**One sentence:** We are a design agency that uses the best AI tools on the market so you get top-quality work without overpaying.

**Three sentences:** We pair veteran designers with enterprise-grade AI like Claude, Gemini, and Midjourney. This lets us deliver branding, social media, web design, and video at the speed and quality of a large agency. But without the large agency price tag.

---

## Voice Pillars

### 1. Results first
Lead with what the client gets. Not what we do. Not how we do it. What they walk away with.

- Good: "Your next campaign, delivered tomorrow."
- Bad: "We leverage cutting-edge AI to optimize your creative workflow."

### 2. Confident, not arrogant
We know our craft. We use the best tools. We do not apologize for using AI. We explain why it is an advantage.

- Good: "We run Claude, Gemini, and Midjourney. The same models Fortune 500 teams pay six figures to access."
- Bad: "We humbly believe our AI-enhanced approach might help improve your brand."

### 3. Transparent about AI
Do not hide AI. Do not oversell it. Position it as an accelerant for human creativity. Our designers make the decisions. AI makes them faster.

- Good: "AI handles the heavy lifting. Our designers handle the thinking."
- Bad: "Our revolutionary AI automatically generates stunning designs."

### 4. Direct and anti-corporate
Short sentences. No jargon. Speak like a smart founder talking to another smart founder. Get to the point.

- Good: "No contracts. No hidden fees. Pause or cancel anytime."
- Bad: "Our flexible subscription model offers unparalleled scalability and transparent billing solutions."

### 5. Premium but accessible
The quality is agency-tier. The price is not. That is the entire value proposition. Never let the reader forget it.

- Good: "Agency-grade creative. Startup-friendly pricing."
- Bad: "Affordable designs for budget-conscious entrepreneurs."

## Writing Rules

### Always do

1. **Use short sentences.** If you can split it, split it.
2. **Name the AI tools.** Say "Claude, Gemini, Midjourney" instead of "AI tools." Specificity builds trust.
3. **Lead with the outcome.** What does the client get? Say that first. Explain how second.
4. **Use numbers.** "$24/mo" is stronger than "affordable." "24 hours" is stronger than "fast."
5. **Be honest.** If something has a limitation, say it. Transparency builds more trust than spin.
6. **Use active voice.** "We deliver" not "designs are delivered."
7. **Write like you talk.** Read it out loud. If it sounds like a press release, rewrite it.

### Never do

1. **Never use em dashes.** Use periods, commas, or line breaks instead. No exceptions.
2. **Never use jargon.** No "leverage," "synergy," "holistic," "ecosystem," "disrupt," or "game-changing."
3. **Never use fluff words.** No "unleash," "elevate," "transform," "cutting-edge," "revolutionary," or "next-generation."
4. **Never be vague about AI.** Do not say "AI-enhanced" without explaining which AI and why it matters.
5. **Never apologize for using AI.** It is our competitive advantage, not something to hide.
6. **Never sound desperate.** No "limited time offer," "act now," or "don't miss out." We are confident, not pushy.
7. **Never use passive voice.** "Designs are created by our team" should be "Our team creates designs."
8. **Never write long paragraphs.** Three sentences max per paragraph. If it needs more, break it up.
9. **Never use exclamation marks in body copy.** Headlines can occasionally use one. Body copy never.
10. **Never call ourselves a "subscription service."** We are an agency. The subscription is just how billing works.

| Word | Why |
|---|---|

| veteran designers | communicates experience without being vague |
| enterprise-grade AI | separates us from people using free AI tools |

| elevate | fluff |
| empower | fluff |

## How We Talk About AI

This is critical. Our relationship with AI is our core differentiator. Here is how to frame it.

### The framework

1. **We invest in AI.** We pay for the best models. Claude, Gemini, Midjourney, Sora, Kling AI. These are not free tools. Name them.
2. **AI accelerates our designers.** It does not replace them. A designer still makes every creative decision. AI just makes execution faster.
3. **The savings go to the client.** Because AI makes us faster, we can charge less. That is the business model. Be transparent about it.
4. **We are not an AI tool.** We are a team of humans who use AI. There is a difference. A big one.

### Good examples

- "We invest in top-tier AI models like Claude, Gemini, and Midjourney. These are not free tools. They are enterprise-grade engines that let our designers deliver production-ready assets in seconds instead of days."
- "AI handles the heavy lifting. Our designers handle the thinking."
- "The AI we use is not cheap, but your subscription is."

### Bad examples

- "Our AI creates stunning designs automatically." (Makes it sound like there are no humans involved.)
- "We use AI-enhanced workflows." (Vague. Which AI? Enhanced how?)
- "Powered by the latest AI technology." (Says nothing specific. Every company says this.)

## How We Talk About Price

Our pricing is our second biggest differentiator. Here is how to frame it.

### The framework

1. **Compare to alternatives.** Agencies charge $5K to $15K a month. A full-time designer costs $60K+ a year. We start at $24/mo. Let the numbers speak.
2. **Explain why we are affordable.** It is not because we cut corners. It is because AI makes us faster. Be transparent.
3. **Never say cheap.** We are not cheap. We are fairly priced. The quality is premium. The pricing model is just smarter.
4. **Use specific numbers.** "$24/mo" always beats "affordable pricing." "24-hour turnaround" always beats "fast delivery."

### Good examples

- "Agencies charge $5K to $15K a month. We deliver the same quality of work starting at $24/mo."
- "The AI we use is not cheap, but your subscription is."
- "Premium AI-powered creative. One flat fee."

### Bad examples

- "Budget-friendly design services." (Makes us sound low quality.)
- "The most affordable design solution on the market." (We are not competing on price alone.)
- "Great design at a fraction of the cost." (Vague. Fraction of what?)

## Headlines

### How to write a 30PX headline

1. **Be specific.** "Results, not promises." is better than "Great Design Services."
2. **Make it punchy.** 2 to 7 words. No more.
3. **Lead with the benefit.** What does the reader care about? Start there.
4. **Use periods for emphasis.** "The math is simple." hits harder than "The math is simple"

### Good headlines
- "Results, not promises."
- "The math is simple."
- "See how we actually work."
- "One team. Every creative discipline."
- "Don't take our word for it."
- "Agency-quality design. AI-powered speed."

### Bad headlines
- "Unleashing the Power of AI-Driven Design" (jargon, fluff, vague)
- "Your One-Stop Shop for All Things Creative" (cliche)
- "Transforming Brands with Cutting-Edge Technology" (every company says this)

## Descriptions and Body Copy

### How to write body copy

1. **Three sentences max per paragraph.**
2. **First sentence states the point. Second sentence supports it. Third sentence drives it home.**
3. **Use concrete details.** Name the tools. Show the numbers. Cite the results.
4. **End sections with a clear takeaway.** The reader should know what to do next.

### Good body copy
> Algorithms reward fresh content. Your audience craves it. The brands winning right now ship creative daily, not monthly.
>
> We pair experienced designers with enterprise-grade AI. Claude for strategy, Midjourney for visuals, Sora for video. You get agency-quality output at a pace and price that actually make sense.

### Bad body copy
> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, brands need to constantly evolve their creative output to stay ahead of the competition. Our innovative AI-powered platform leverages cutting-edge technology to deliver seamless, high-quality design solutions that empower businesses to transform their visual identity and elevate their brand presence across all channels.

## CTAs (Calls to Action)

### Rules
1. **Be specific about what happens next.** "Start for $24/mo" is better than "Get started."
2. **Keep it under 5 words.**
3. **No pressure language.** No "Act now" or "Limited time."

### Good CTAs
- "Start for $24/mo"
- "See pricing"
- "See more work"
- "Book a meeting"
- "Start your subscription"

### Bad CTAs
- "Unlock your creative potential today"
- "Begin your transformative journey"
- "Discover the future of design"

## Social Media Voice

When writing for social media, keep the same voice but dial up the casual and dial down the formal. Social posts should feel like a smart friend sharing something useful.

### Rules for social
1. No hashtag spam. Two max per post.
2. No emoji walls. One or two emojis per post, if any.
3. Short paragraphs. One to two sentences each.
4. Lead with the hook. First line must stop the scroll.
5. End with a clear action or thought. No trailing off.

### Good social post
> Agencies charge $5K to $15K a month for what we deliver starting at $24.
>
> The difference? We use Claude, Gemini, and Midjourney to move faster.
>
> Same quality. Smarter process. Fair price.

### Bad social post
> 🚀🔥 We're THRILLED to announce our AMAZING new AI-powered design subscription!!! 💥✨ Unleash your brand's FULL POTENTIAL with our cutting-edge creative solutions! 🎨💪 Don't miss out on this game-changing opportunity! Link in bio! 🔗👇

## Email Voice

Emails should be the most personal version of our voice. Write as if you are sending a message to one specific person, not blasting a list.

### Rules for email
1. First name in the greeting. "Hey Sarah" not "Dear valued customer."
2. Get to the point in the first sentence.
3. One idea per email. Do not cram.
4. End with one clear CTA. Not three.
5. Keep subject lines under 6 words. No clickbait.

## Quick Reference Checklist

Before publishing any piece of content, run through this:

- [ ] Does it lead with a result or benefit?
- [ ] Are there zero em dashes?
- [ ] Are there zero fluff words (elevate, unleash, transform, cutting-edge)?
- [ ] Is every paragraph three sentences or fewer?
- [ ] Did you name specific AI tools instead of saying "AI-enhanced"?
- [ ] Would a busy founder understand this in 5 seconds?
- [ ] Does it sound like a human wrote it, not a press release generator?
- [ ] Are there specific numbers instead of vague claims?
- [ ] Is the CTA clear and under 5 words?
- [ ] Did you avoid calling us a "subscription service"?
